UOAether

An old-school/modern hybrid progression PVE server

play.uoaether.com - port 2593


More info and custom map files available via discord: https://discord.gg/xx2U8xjM3n


Note: This server is currently in BUILDING and TESTING phase. Feel free to join in and help shape the world!

01

Character Creation

A few things work differently the moment you roll a new character.

  • PVE only. PvP is disabled entirely.
  • Human only. Elf and Gargoyle are not available as starting races.
  • Every new character starts with a full spellbook. Regardless of your starting template or skill picks, you begin with a Magery spellbook already containing every spell across all eight circles — no need to train Inscription or track down scrolls just to fill it in.
  • Five skills are disabled shard-wide and cannot be trained by any means: Bushido, Ninjitsu, Spellweaving, Mysticism, and Throwing. They won't appear as usable starting skills, and normal skill gain won't raise them even if you try.
02

PolySpec & Loadouts

Save your current skills and stats as a named "spec," then switch between saved specs whenever you like — no retraining required. Play a warrior one day and a mage or bard the next, all on the same character, without keeping a stable of alts just to try a different playstyle.

Getting started

  • Type [polyspec anywhere in the world to open your spec manager.
  • Each spec slot costs gold to unlock — the cost rises with every additional slot you add.
  • Save your current build into an open slot, then load it back again at any point later.
  • Give each spec its own name, and reorder your saved specs however you'd like them listed.
Switching to a different spec has a short cooldown, so it can't be used to swap builds instantly mid-fight — think of it as changing your loadout between engagements, not during one.

What carries over, and what doesn't

PolySpec only ever touches your raw skill points and your Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence — nothing else about your character is affected by switching.

  • Your Aether progression is completely untouched by a spec switch — it's shared across every spec you own.
  • Any Power Scroll or Stat Scroll increase you've earned benefits every spec, not just the one you were playing when you used it.

The Aether System

A custom progression system layered on top of normal character advancement — a second, parallel form of growth alongside your skills and stats.

How it works

  • You attune to two aethers at once — a Primary and a Secondary — from the types above.
  • Note: Your Secondary aether will be reduced by 50% magnitude vs primary. Example: If your bonus was 20% damage bonus as primary, it will only be 10% damage bonus if slotted as a secondary aether.
  • Each aether type levels independently from 0 to 15. Progress in a type is saved even when it's not currently equipped — level Terra to 10, switch to Tempest, and your Terra progress is exactly where you left it when you switch back.
  • You level an aether by killing creatures. Kills grant experience toward your current level and a chance to drop an Aether Vial matching one of the aether types — used to advance that specific aether directly. Rarely, a Prismatic Aether Vial drops instead, which can be exchanged for any type of your choosing.
  • As you level, you unlock passive bonuses (like weapon accuracy and damage resistance from your Primary), an elemental proc chance in combat, and ability unlocks at certain tiers.
  • Respeccing is currently free — reassign your Primary or Secondary at any time without penalty.
Terra, Prodigy, Tempest, Blaze, Marksman, and Prowl lean offensive. Celestial is a healing specialization. Cadence and Sylvan lean support — Cadence interacts directly with bard skills. Professional is built for crafting, not combat: it boosts exceptional-item chance and gives a chance to not consume materials on a successful craft. See Bard Skills.

Accessing it

Type [aether to open your Aether Attunement screen. It shows your banked level in every aether type — not just your current two — lets you reassign your Primary/Secondary slots, and breaks down your current bonuses. A minimum resolution of 1920×1080 is recommended for the panel to display properly.

04

Perks

A separate, permanent progression system layered on top of everything else. Where Aether can meaningfully build up over weeks, Perks is a long-term grind — built to take a casual player years to fully max out.

How it works

  • There are 14 Perk categories, each covering a small, permanent combat or utility bonus — see the table below.
  • Each category has 10 levels. Every kill grants experience toward whichever category you're currently Training — only one category earns experience at a time, and you choose which.
  • Once a category banks enough experience, its next level becomes eligible — but you still have to spend 10 Aether Shards to actually unlock it. Experience is never lost or wasted while it waits to be claimed.
  • Unlocked levels don't have to be active. You can hold up to 10 total active levels across all 14 categories combined, in whatever split you like — five in one category and five in another, or ten stacked into a single one.
  • Switching your Training target, or which levels are active, is completely free — no cost, no cooldown. The only restriction is that changes can only be made while standing in a safe area (your own house, or inside a guarded town).

Type [perks to open your Perks panel. It shows every category's progress, lets you set your Training target, spend Aether Shards to unlock eligible levels, and choose which unlocked levels are currently active.

05

Bonus Tracker

A live readout of every combat and utility bonus currently affecting your character — gear, Aether, and Perks, all combined into your real current numbers, calculated fresh each time you open it. Handy for actually seeing what a gear swap or a Perk reshuffle does before you commit to it.

What it shows

Two tabs:

  • Combat — Chance to Hit, Swing Speed, Weapon Damage Increase, Damage Multiplier, and Damage Resistance.
  • Modifiers — everything else: Regeneration, Stat & Resource Bonuses, Utility, Casting, and Resistances.
It's self-view only — the panel shows your own numbers, not another player's or a monster's.

Accessing it

Type [bonuses (or its alias [mystats) to open the panel at any time.

06

Combat Mechanics

A couple of underlying combat mechanics work differently here than you might expect from stock Ultima Online.

Archery

All Archery weapons — both Bows and Crossbows — swing 40% faster than other weapon types, an innate bonus baked into the weapon family itself. It stacks with every other swing-speed bonus you have from gear, Aether, and Perks.

Weapon Timing

Unequipping a weapon doesn't reset its swing timer — the classic UO "instahit" mechanic is back. If your timer is already counting down when you unequip, it keeps ticking regardless of what's in your hands; re-equip once it's fully expired and your very next swing lands instantly.

For a clean instahit, step fully out of combat and wait it out unarmed. Throwing bare-handed swings at a target while you wait can overwrite the timer with your unarmed speed instead.
07

Tank, DPS & Threat

A real threat system now drives monster targeting shard-wide, replacing the old "whoever's closest or hit me last" logic. Pets and summons can be assigned a combat role — Tank, Ranged, or Melee — right from the Animal Lore panel.

  • A pet or summon set to Tank generates dramatically more threat and will hold aggro on its own, even without Taunt.
  • Ranged and Melee generate far less threat, letting them fight alongside a tank without accidentally stealing the monster's attention.
  • Taunt (on the pets that have it) now forces a guaranteed threat spike plus a short lock window where the target can't be peeled away no matter what happens next — a real panic button, not just a coin flip.
  • Set a role any time from your pet or summon's Animal Lore panel — it takes effect immediately and is remembered for next time.
Healing threat isn't factored in yet — right now only damage builds threat, so an eager healer can still pull aggro off the tank. A more complete version of the system is planned.
08

Companions & Taming

Taming carries a significant custom layer on top of stock pet mechanics.

Custom pet roster

14 unique tameable creatures exist alongside the usual wild animals, split across three combat roles:

  • Tank — built to hold aggro and soak damage (e.g. EmberWarden, Goatox)
  • Ranged — casters and archers that hold distance (e.g. TempestDrake, Mysticwyrm, Sagefang)
  • Melee — straightforward damage dealers (e.g. Bloodfang)

A number of elemental-themed pairs (fire, ice, poison, storm, and more) exist without a fixed role.

Pet leveling

Custom pets gain their own levels — separate from their stock stats — as they fight beside you, earning points to spend into their Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence. How high a pet can level is tied to your Animal Taming skill: higher skill raises the cap your pets can reach. If you want to change how a pet's points were spent, a respec option is available.

To check a pet's progress, use your Animal Lore skill on it — or use the Lore option available for stabled pets — to see its current level, experience, and role.

Managing your pets

  • Speak "stable" to a Stable Master to open your claim list and pick a pet.
  • Speak "stable all" to stable every pet currently following you in one go.
  • Speaking "claim" alone also opens the claim picker.
  • [PetPull teleports all your pets to your current location after a short countdown — also available from your own right-click menu as "Pull Pets."
09

Summoner Builds

Magery's summon spells got real attention: Fire Elemental and Earth Elemental have been reworked into stronger, more reliable companions — several bugs that left summons spawning anywhere from 71% to nearly 200% of their intended health are fixed. The other four Magery summon types haven't been touched yet.

  • Spirit Speak is the summoner's real investment skill now: every full 10 points adds 1% summon power (capped at 12% at 120 skill) and 2 minutes of duration. 100 Spirit Speak = +10% power and 20 minutes.
  • Monsters can no longer dispel your summons, whether by melee contact or spellcasting — dispel can now only come from another player's Dispel or Mass Dispel spell.
  • Casting a 7th or 8th Circle Magery spell at GM skill (100) now always succeeds — a quality-of-life change for every high-end caster, not just summoners.
  • Mounts cost 0 follower slots on this shard, so riding one never competes with your summons for space.
  • Fire Elemental and Earth Elemental can be set to a Tank, Ranged, or Melee role too — see Tank, DPS & Threat.
The trade-off: dismiss your summons and you get +15% spell damage as a pure caster instead. Switch back any time — it costs nothing either way.
10

Bard Skills

Peacemaking, Provocation, and Discordance work as normal, but the underlying difficulty and landing-chance math have been recalibrated — numbers you remember from stock UO or other shards won't line up exactly.

  • Effective creature difficulty for bard purposes is scaled up from stock.
  • The random landing-chance window is wider than stock — a well-trained bard has more consistent success against tougher targets than you might expect.
  • With Cadence aether, successfully landing a peace, provoke, or discord grants a bonus effect on top of the normal result — improved odds, longer duration, and an extra payoff on a landed success.

Notable Loot

A couple of drop types worth knowing about, since they don't work quite like stock Ultima Online.

Power Scrolls

Power Scrolls drop from ordinary kills shard-wide — not just from champion spawns or specific bosses. Tougher creatures have a meaningfully better chance: the drop odds scale directly with how dangerous a creature is. The scroll pool itself has also been reshaped for this shard — some skills that never had Power Scrolls in stock Ultima Online (Carpentry, Tinkering, Fletching, Inscription) now do, and a handful of skills that don't exist on this shard at all were removed from the pool entirely.

Aether Shards

A separate, rarer drop that can appear on the same kills as Power Scrolls, following the same difficulty-based odds. What Aether Shards are actually for is still being decided — right now they're a valuable curiosity worth holding onto rather than a currency with a fixed use yet. (Not to be confused with Aether Vials, the currency used for the Aether attunement system above — Shards and Vials are two separate items.)

12

The Item Shop (Gold Sink Store)

A gold-currency store for spending large sums on account-wide conveniences, cosmetics, and quality-of-life items — replacing the old account-age veteran reward system, folded directly into the store.

  • The full former veteran rewards catalog — dye tubs, statues, tools, mounts, and more — now purchasable with gold instead of gated by account age.
  • Custom shard items, including a Portable Repair Bench, an Ethereal Lasher mount, a Bag of Sending, a Clothing Bless Deed, a Special Dye Tub, and a Wall Safe Deed.
Cloak and robe rewards are intentionally excluded from the store, different options planned.

The store opens the same way as on any official Ultima Online client — through your client's Help menu / in-game store option. Prices are set in gold rather than real currency.

13

Transmog

Make any equippable item borrow the look of another item you own, while keeping its own stats, name, resistances, and properties completely untouched. Free, and doesn't consume the item you're copying the look from.

  • Type [xmog to open the appearance picker.
  • The borrowed look only shows while the item is worn — in your pack or on a corpse, it shows its real appearance.
  • The item you're copying the look from must be blessed. You can choose to also borrow its hue, or keep your own.
  • Works across compatible slots: the same layer, or matching groups (any torso-layer piece onto any other torso-layer piece, same for legs). Shields can borrow other shields' looks; weapons can't be used this way, since weapon art drives your swing animation.
  • A few special appearance-only pieces exist that hide a slot entirely — the item stays equipped and keeps its stats, it just renders nothing.
Still in testing — not available on the live server yet.
14

Wall Safes

A Wall Safe is a placeable house item, purchasable from the item shop, that lets you access your bank box remotely by simply double-clicking it — no travel to an actual bank required to check your balance or move items in and out.

World & Dungeons

Several custom regions and dungeons exist beyond the standard map, each with tiered difficulty — typically Medium → Hard → Elite → Legendary.

Insidious Dungeon

A mid-sized dungeon with a full difficulty spread from Medium through Legendary.

Ignis

A fire-themed dungeon, similarly spread across all four difficulty tiers.

Underworld

Currently offers Medium and Hard content. Its harder Miniboss/Boss tiers and the paired Stygian Abyss dungeon are still in development. (NOT SPAWNED)

Wilderness

By far the largest area, split into five zones: Normal, Desert, Snow, Swamp, and Castle Dissent. Castle Dissent skips the lower tiers entirely, offering only Elite and Legendary content.

Radius

A corrupted city overrun by Castle Dissent's knight order, where friendly town vendors have been replaced by hostile "Dissent" versions of every classic vendor trade.

Not everything out there shows up on a map. Some of the more remote corners of the world are rumored to hold something far tougher than the usual wildlife — you won't get a warning before you stumble into it.
16

Champion Spawns

Champion spawn altars work as normal, but the total kills needed to fully clear one from start to Champion has been substantially reduced from stock — spawns are noticeably faster and more accessible than you may be used to.

17

Quests

A growing set of area-based kill quests are available from quest-giver NPCs scattered around the world. Accept one by speaking with the NPC, then head into the specified area and start clearing it out — kills within the quest's territory count automatically, no special targeting required.

Type [quest at any time to open your personal quest log — a combined list of everything you've currently accepted, with live progress, where to turn in, and an option to abandon a quest if you'd rather drop it.

Crafting quests are in the pipeline — a parallel system where you'll turn in crafted items instead of kills — but they aren't live yet. Every quest available in-game right now is a kill quest.
18

Fusion Crafting

Blacksmithy, Carpentry, and Fletching all use a custom crafting menu in place of the stock one — build weapons and armor with hand-picked properties instead of random rolls. Tinkering and every other trade are untouched and still work as normal.

How it works

  • Double-click your smithing hammer, saw, or fletching tool to open the custom crafting menu instead of the usual crafting window.
  • Pick your item, then spend Shards to add properties from a list — Hit Chance Increase, Spell Damage Increase, Weapon Damage Increase, Swing Speed Increase, and more. Each property has its own Shard cost and cap, and you choose exactly what goes on the item.
  • A crafted item starts from a completely clean slate — no random elemental damage split, no stray stock bonuses riding along underneath. What you pick is what you get, nothing more.
  • No craft timer, and you can craft even while overweight.
  • Weapon Damage Increase and Swing Speed Increase can now be picked on armor too, not just weapons — real Ultima Online mechanics, both stack across everything you have equipped toward your total.

Sockets & Gems

Weapons and helmets can hold Fusion Gems — extra socket slots that add further bonuses on top of whatever you crafted. Double-click a gem, then target the weapon or helmet you want to socket it into; a panel opens showing your slots, letting you fill an empty one or replace what's already there.

Gem acquisition isn't live yet — they aren't dropping or craftable through normal play at this time. The socketing system itself is fully working, just not the part where you'd normally get one.
19

Crafting Changes

Several stock crafting options have been intentionally removed across almost every trade — largely Elven and Gargoyle-race items (since only Humans exist on this shard), most Tokuno/Samurai Empire-era armor and weapon variants, and a number of exotic late-game artifact weapons, armor pieces, and potions.

If something you remember being craftable on another shard isn't available here, it was most likely removed deliberately rather than missing by mistake.

Restricted to classic recipes only

Tailoring and Tinkering are both restricted to classic, Second Age-era recipes only — newer expansion-gated patterns are not craftable. Tinkering keeps one deliberate exception: magic jewelry (the gem-based bracelet and ring sets) is still fully craftable despite everything else in that era being stripped.

Currently unavailable entirely

Glassblowing and Masonry are both switched off shard-wide for now — attempting to craft from either will tell you that you haven't learned the trade, regardless of your actual skill level. This is a temporary state, not a permanent removal.

Bulk Order Deed rewards

Turning in Bulk Order Deeds still works as normal, but the reward pool has changed: Talismans and Power Scrolls are no longer offered as BOD turn-in rewards in any trade. In their place, every trade now offers a single Aether Vial reward option instead — the same currency used by the Aether attunement system. This is an early pass and the reward pool is expected to keep evolving.

Travel & Movement

  • Public moongates in Felucca(BROKEN CURRENTLY SORRY) lead to shard-specific destinations rather than the standard city list: Conim Bank, Conim Moongate, Cido Entrance, Insidious Dungeon Entrance, Ignis Entrance, Radius Bank, and Radius Moongate. Moongates on every other facet — Trammel, Ilshenar, Malas, Tokuno, TerMur — still lead to their normal stock destinations.
  • The "stuck" menu always sends you to Felucca, to a fixed point near Conim, regardless of which facet or town you were in when you used it — it will not return you to your race's usual home city.
  • Teleport ropes, a custom item type found in parts of the world, can be used even while you're a ghost, not just while alive.

Swift Footwear

A line of enchanted footwear — Swift Sandals, Swift Shoes, Swift Thigh Boots, and Swift Boots — that grants permanent mount-speed movement for as long as it's worn, no toggling required. All four pieces are blessed (won't be lost on death) and dyeable, so you can recolor them to match your outfit.
