A Custom Ultima Online Shard

UOAether

What's different from stock Ultima Online — the systems that exist on this shard, what they do, and how to reach them in-game.

01

Character Creation

A few things work differently the moment you roll a new character.

  • Human only. Elf and Gargoyle are not available as starting races.
  • Every new character starts with a full spellbook. Regardless of your starting template or skill picks, you begin with a Magery spellbook already containing every spell across all eight circles — no need to train Inscription or track down scrolls just to fill it in.
  • Five skills are disabled shard-wide and cannot be trained by any means: Bushido, Ninjitsu, Spellweaving, Mysticism, and Throwing. They won't appear as usable starting skills, and normal skill gain won't raise them even if you try.
02

The Aether System

A custom progression system layered on top of normal character advancement — a second, parallel form of growth alongside your skills and stats.

TerraMelee
CadenceBard
CelestialHealing
EldritchMage
SylvanTamer
TempestUtility

How it works

  • You attune to two aethers at once — a Primary and a Secondary — from the six types above. More are planned.
  • Each aether type levels independently from 0 to 15. Progress in a type is saved even when it's not currently equipped — level Terra to 10, switch to Tempest, and your Terra progress is exactly where you left it when you switch back.
  • You level an aether by killing creatures. Kills grant experience toward your current level and a chance to drop Aether Vials, a currency used to advance your aether directly.
  • As you level, you unlock passive bonuses (like weapon accuracy or damage resistance from your Primary), an elemental proc chance in combat, and ability unlocks at certain tiers.
  • Respeccing is currently free — reassign your Primary or Secondary at any time without penalty.
Terra, Eldritch, and Tempest lean offensive with stronger combat procs. Celestial is a healing specialization, so it sits apart from that offensive lean despite its stronger proc. Cadence and Sylvan lean support — Cadence in particular interacts directly with bard skills. See Bard Skills.

Accessing it

Type [aether to open your Aether Attunement screen. It shows your banked level in every aether type — not just your current two — lets you reassign your Primary/Secondary slots, and breaks down your current bonuses. A minimum resolution of 1920×1080 is recommended for the panel to display properly.

03

Companions & Taming

Taming carries a significant custom layer on top of stock pet mechanics.

Custom pet roster

14 unique tameable creatures exist alongside the usual wild animals, split across three combat roles:

  • Tank — built to hold aggro and soak damage (e.g. EmberWarden, Goatox)
  • Ranged — casters and archers that hold distance (e.g. TempestDrake, Mysticwyrm, Sagefang)
  • Melee — straightforward damage dealers (e.g. Bloodfang)

A number of elemental-themed pairs (fire, ice, poison, storm, and more) exist without a fixed role, and can fit whatever part your party needs.

Pet leveling

Custom pets gain their own levels — separate from their stock stats — as they fight beside you, earning points to spend into their Strength, Dexterity, and Intelligence. How high a pet can level is tied to your Animal Taming skill: higher skill raises the cap your pets can reach. If you want to change how a pet's points were spent, a respec option is available.

To check a pet's progress, use your Animal Lore skill on it — or use the Lore option available for stabled pets — to see its current level, experience, and role.

Managing your pets

  • Speak "stable" to a Stable Master to open your claim list and pick a pet.
  • Speak "stable all" to stable every pet currently following you in one go.
  • Speaking "claim" alone also opens the claim picker.
  • [PetPull teleports all your pets to your current location after a short countdown — also available from your own right-click menu as "Pull Pets."
04

Bard Skills

Peacemaking, Provocation, and Discordance work as normal, but the underlying difficulty and landing-chance math have been recalibrated — numbers you remember from stock UO or other shards won't line up exactly.

  • Effective creature difficulty for bard purposes is scaled up from stock.
  • The random landing-chance window is wider than stock — a well-trained bard has more consistent success against tougher targets than you might expect.
  • If your Cadence aether is your Secondary, successfully landing a peace, provoke, or discord grants a bonus effect on top of the normal result — improved odds, longer duration, and an extra payoff on a landed success.
05

The Item Shop (Gold Sink Store)

A gold-currency store for spending large sums on account-wide conveniences, cosmetics, and quality-of-life items — this replaces the old account-age veteran reward system entirely, folding it directly into the store instead.

  • The full former veteran rewards catalog — dye tubs, statues, tools, mounts, and more — now purchasable with gold instead of gated by account age.
  • Custom shard items, including a Portable Repair Bench, an Ethereal Lasher mount, a Bag of Sending, a Clothing Bless Deed, a Special Dye Tub, and a Wall Safe Deed.
Cloak and robe rewards are intentionally excluded from the store, since this shard doesn't allow clothing to carry combat-affecting stat bonuses.

The store opens the same way as on any official Ultima Online client — through your client's Help menu / in-game store option. Prices are set in gold rather than real currency.

06

Wall Safes

A Wall Safe is a placeable house item, purchasable from the item shop, that lets you access your bank box remotely by simply double-clicking it — no travel to an actual bank required to check your balance or move items in and out.

07

World & Dungeons

Several custom regions and dungeons exist beyond the standard map, each with tiered difficulty — typically Medium → Hard → Elite → Legendary.

Insidious Dungeon

A mid-sized dungeon with a full difficulty spread from Medium through Legendary.

Ignis

A fire-themed dungeon, similarly spread across all four difficulty tiers.

Underworld

Currently offers Medium and Hard content. Its harder Miniboss/Boss tiers and the paired Stygian Abyss dungeon are still in development. (NOT SPAWNED)

Wilderness

By far the largest area, split into five zones: Normal, Desert, Snow, Swamp, and Castle Dissent. Castle Dissent skips the lower tiers entirely, offering only Elite and Legendary content — a fallen knight order theme with human-bodied, armored enemies.

Radius

A corrupted city overrun by Castle Dissent's knight order, where friendly town vendors have been replaced by hostile "Dissent" versions of every classic vendor trade. Set up as a future liberation event once the server has a second town worth freeing.

08

Champion Spawns

Champion spawn altars work as normal, but the total kills needed to fully clear one from start to Champion has been substantially reduced from stock — spawns are noticeably faster and more accessible than you may be used to.

09

Crafting Changes

Several stock crafting options have been intentionally removed from the Carpentry and Blacksmithing menus — largely Elven and Gargoyle-race items (since only Humans exist on this shard), most Tokuno/Samurai Empire-era armor and weapon variants, and a number of exotic late-game artifact weapons and armor pieces.

If something you remember being craftable on another shard isn't available here, it was most likely removed deliberately rather than missing by mistake.

Tailoring is restricted to classic, Second Age-era recipes only — newer expansion-gated clothing and armor patterns are not craftable.

10

Travel & Movement

  • Public moongates in Felucca lead to shard-specific destinations rather than the standard city list: Conim Bank, Conim Moongate, Cido Entrance, Insidious Dungeon Entrance, Ignis Entrance, Radius Bank, and Radius Moongate. Moongates on every other facet — Trammel, Ilshenar, Malas, Tokuno, TerMur — still lead to their normal stock destinations.
  • The "stuck" menu always sends you to Felucca, to a fixed point near Conim, regardless of which facet or town you were in when you used it — it will not return you to your race's usual home city.
  • Teleport ropes, a custom item type found in parts of the world, can be used even while you're a ghost, not just while alive.
